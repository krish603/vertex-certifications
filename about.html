Who we are

One partner for certification, compliance & growth

Vertex Certification is a one-stop consulting partner for ISO certifications, regulatory compliance, business funding, recruitment and management advisory. We help startups, MSMEs and established enterprises meet global standards and unlock sustainable growth.

Our services span ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, 27001 and 22000, CE marking, GMP, HACCP and FSSAI approvals, along with company registration, trademark, GST and legal compliance. On the growth side we deliver business loans, government grants, MSME subsidies, staffing solutions and end-to-end management consulting.

With a team of certified auditors, chartered accountants, HR specialists and industry consultants, we simplify complex processes and deliver measurable results.
